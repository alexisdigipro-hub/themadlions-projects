03 · For the set

Made the way a call sheet is read.

At five in the morning, on a phone, in a hurry.

The calendar

The day, in their calendar.

One tap on the ordino puts the shoot day in the phone's calendar, with the call time and the place.

The look

Dark, Glass or Light, your colour.

Pick a look, an accent colour and a cover picture with the production's logo. The ordino looks like your project, not like a form. Put the sections in the order you want, with your own titles.

DarkGlassLight
The next day

Duplicate, change the date.

Day two is day one with a few changes: Duplicate, on the list or in the editor, makes a new ordino with everything in it, ready for the new date and not sent until you send it.

The family

Straight into the crew's chat.

Send the ordino into a RoarMe chat, or by WhatsApp, Viber or SMS. It is the same RoarMe account, so every ordino you make is in one place, on any device. In the THEMADLIONS hub, the team’s ordinos live with its projects instead.

07 · Questions

Good to know.

Is Roardino free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Does the crew need an account or an app?

No. They open the link in the phone's browser. Only you, who make the ordino, sign in, with your RoarMe account.

What if the call changes?

Change it in Roardino and press Send link again. The link stays the same, so whoever opens it sees the new call.

Where do the weather and the sunset come from?

From the place and the date of the ordino: Roardino finds the spot from the address or from a Google Maps link. The forecast reaches about two weeks ahead, with the chance of rain; further out, you still get sunrise and sunset.

How do I send it?

Press Send link: copy the link, or share it from the phone to WhatsApp, Viber, SMS or anywhere. Inside RoarMe, it goes straight into the chat as a card.

Is Roardino in the THEMADLIONS hub?

Yes, for the team: it is in the hub’s Outbox, on the hub’s own projects and accounts, and each project’s Overview opens its ordino.
