01 · Only here

Three things only Roardino does.

Why a production stops sending the call sheet as a PDF in the group chat.

01

One link, every phone.

The ordino is a page, not a file: it opens at once on any phone, with no app and no zooming. Change the call at night and the same link shows the new one in the morning.

The look

Dark, Glass or Light, your colour.

The next day

Duplicate, change the date.

Day two is day one with a few changes: Duplicate makes a new ordino with everything in it, ready for the new date, as a draft until you send it.

The family

Straight into the crew's chat.

Send the ordino into a RoarMe chat, or by WhatsApp, Viber or SMS. It is the same RoarMe account, so every ordino you make is in one place, on any device.

05 · Questions

Good to know.

Is Roardino free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Does the crew need an account or an app?

No. They open the link in the phone's browser. Only you, who make the ordino, sign in, with your RoarMe account.

What if the call changes?

Change it in Roardino. The link stays the same, so whoever opens it sees the new call.

Where do the weather and the sunset come from?

From the place and the date of the ordino. Paste a Google Maps link, or let Roardino find the spot from the address, and they fill in by themselves.

How do I send it?

Copy the link, or send it by WhatsApp, Viber or SMS, or into a RoarMe chat, from the ordino itself.
