04 · For the shoot

Made for a shoot's files.

The card, the stills, the edit, the deliveries: all at once.

Where from

Drop whole folders.

One file, a few, or a folder with everything inside: RoarMorph finds the pictures, videos and sound in it. Pictures go three at a time, videos one by one, with the progress of each.

Reframe

9:16, square, 4:5 or 2.39:1.

Turn a 16:9 shot into a Reel, a post, a story or a scope frame: cropped, or whole with its own blurred copy behind. Or a size of your own, with bars where the shape differs. Anamorphic footage is desqueezed on the way, 1.33× to 2×.

Three presets

Small, balanced, or quality.

One tap sets everything; or set the quality, the size, the frame rate, the codec and the speed yourself. RoarMorph remembers what you chose.

For the edit

ProRes when it has to be.

The phone's HEVC or a web download into ProRes (Proxy to 4444) or DNxHR (LB to 444) for the timeline, MXF for Avid, XDCAM for broadcast, FFV1 for a lossless archive, a still for the poster, the interview as 24-bit WAV for the mix.

RoarMorph, briefly.

01Pictures in

Keep only a part of the clip: from 0:12 to 0:45, say.

05A still

One frame as JPG or PNG, at the moment you say.

06The sound only

MP3, M4A, WAV 16 or 24-bit, AIFF, ALAC, FLAC or OGG out of any video.

H.264 and H.265 with the Mac's own video engine, several times faster; if it says no, the software one makes the same file.

A LUT (.cube, .3dl, .dat, .csp) on every clip, and HDR from the iPhone (HLG) or HDR10 brought to Rec.709, so it does not look grey.

09Loudness

−14 LUFS for the web, −16 for a podcast, −23 for TV; mono or stereo; 44.1 or 48 kHz.

10English and Greek

The app speaks both; choose at the top.

11GIF both ways

A clip into a GIF or an animated WebP with its own palette, and a GIF into MP4, MOV, ProRes or WebM.

12A size of your own

Width × height, with bars where the shape differs, and a keyframe every N frames.

13Your metadata, your call

Camera data and location are left out by default; one switch keeps them.

14Stop any time

Stop one file or all of them; what was half made is left out.

08 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarMorph free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no watermark.

How much smaller does a file get?

It depends on the file. A ProRes master to H.265 is often 90 to 97% smaller; a PNG to WebP 70 to 95%; a phone photo to JPG about 40%. Each file in the list shows how much it saved.

Does the target size land exactly?

With Two passes, yes, just under it: in our tests a 1 MB target came out at 0.96 MB. One pass is faster and lands close. A bitrate in Mb/s works the same way, as Resolve’s “Restrict to”.

Which format for Instagram, email or the web?

MP4 with H.264 plays everywhere; H.265 is about half the size and plays on every phone and recent computer. For pictures on a website, WebP or AVIF.

Does it open RAW and HEIC photos?

Yes on a Mac: HEIC from the iPhone and camera RAW (DNG, CR3, NEF, ARW, RAF and more) open through the Mac's own tools, and HEIC can be saved too. On Windows HEIC may not open and RAW not yet; convert those on a Mac.

Can it turn a GIF into a video?

Yes, since 0.3.1: a GIF counts as a moving picture, so it can become MP4, MOV, ProRes or WebM. An odd size like 481×271 is made even for H.264.

Why does my iPhone video look grey on other screens?

Because it is HDR (HLG). HDR to Rec.709 is on by default and works only when the source is HDR, so the new file looks right everywhere.

Do I need the internet?

No. Only to download the app and its updates; every conversion runs on your computer.

A PSD in, every layer out

Drop a Photoshop file and it becomes any picture format, the same on a Mac and on Windows: RGB, grayscale or CMYK, 8, 16 or 32-bit. If it has layers, RoarMorph asks: only the picture, or each layer as its own PNG too, the size of the document, in its place, in a folder beside it.
