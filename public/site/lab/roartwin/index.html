01

Not fooled by names.

02 · For the drives

Made for a shoot's drives.

The card, the backup, the edit, the exports: all at once.

Where to look

Drop folders and drives.

One folder, a few, or whole disks at once: drop them on the window or choose them. The system's folders, the Trash and libraries like Photos or Final Cut are left alone.

The one to keep

The best one, suggested.

For the same video RoarTwin suggests the biggest picture, then the most data. For exact copies, the oldest, the original. One click keeps the suggested one, or choose yourself.

What it is

A picture, and every detail.

Every video and photo with a preview, its folder, size, picture size, length, codec, frame rate and date, side by side. Show it in Finder or Explorer, or open it, from the list.

On paper

The list, for the team.

Save every group as a CSV that Excel and Numbers open: what is a twin of what, the suggested one, what you chose. Decide together before anything moves.

06 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarTwin free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

How does it know two videos are the same?

First by their length, within a second. Then by their pictures: nine frames, from 10% to 90% through, each shrunk to a small fingerprint and compared. If most of them match, it is the same video, whatever its size, codec or container.

Can it delete something by mistake?

It does not delete at all. Chosen files go to the Trash, and one of each group always stays. Empty the Trash only when you are sure.

Is it slow on a big drive?

Listing is fast. Only files of the same size are read, and only those whose start, middle and end match are read in full. Videos are looked at only when another one has about the same length. A full drive of footage can still take a while, and you can stop at any time.

Does it work on external drives and servers?

Yes: any folder your computer can open, a card, a USB or Thunderbolt drive, a network share.

What about RAW camera files?

Exact copies of any file are found, RAW clips like R3D or BRAW too. Comparing the pictures needs a format ffmpeg can read: MOV, MP4, MXF, MKV, AVI, MTS and the like.
