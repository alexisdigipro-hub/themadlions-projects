Experiment 01 Live · beta

ROARME

The messenger for teams. Built for the way a crew really talks: fast, with big files, and sometimes in private.

  • No phone number. Just your @username.
  • RoarTransfer. Files up to 5 GB, with one short link.
  • Encrypted secret chats. On when it matters, with one tap.

Experiment 02 Live · beta

ROARDINO

The call sheet, in one link. Write the call, the location and the day's plan, pick a picture and a colour, and send the crew a page that opens beautifully on any phone.

  • Your own account. Every ordino you make, in one place, from any device.
  • One link for the crew. Directions, a tap to call and the day in their calendar.
  • Weather and sunset. Filled in by themselves for the place and the day.

Experiment 03 Live · beta

ROARMAIL

The mail app for Mac and Windows. Every mailbox in one inbox, at keyboard speed, and no server of ours between you and your mail.

  • All your mail, one inbox. Gmail, Outlook, iCloud or your own domain, side by side.
  • Private by design. Tracking pixels blocked, links checked, passwords in your computer's keychain.
  • Undo everything. Archive, delete, move, even send: a few seconds to change your mind.

Experiment 04 Live · beta

ROARLIST

The shot list, frame by frame. Scenes and shots with size, angle, movement and lens, a picture for every shot, and a PDF the crew can read on set.

  • A frame for every shot. Drop, paste or choose it, and the list turns into a storyboard.
  • One PDF for the crew. As a list or as a storyboard, ready for the set.
  • Your RoarMe account. Every shot list in your cloud, on the web and on Mac and Windows.

Experiment 05 Live

ROARTRANSFER

Send large files, free and secure. Up to 5 GB with one short link, by email or as a link, with a password and an email when it is downloaded. At roartransfer.com, in every RoarMe chat and in RoarMail.

  • 5 GB, as it is. Original quality, real names, several files under one short link.
  • Picks up where it stopped. If the connection drops, it carries on instead of starting again.
  • Gone by itself. After 1 or 3 days the files are erased. No account needed to download, or even to send with FastTransfer.

Experiment 07 New · beta

ROARKIT

One app, sixteen tools: it sees what your computer needs, makes room, copies and checks a shoot's cards, reports every clip, finds the twins, keeps your files tidy and your passwords locked.

  • What the computer needs, first. A score for the disks, memory, heat, battery and caches, each with the tool that sorts it out.
  • Made for the shoot. Card copies checked byte for byte with an ASC MHL, media reports, clip check, contact sheets, renaming.
  • Nothing is lost. Only to the Trash, never written over, and the passwords sealed on your computer.
Discover RoarKit Mac / Windows
Experiment 08 New · beta

ROARVERTER

Converts and compresses pictures, videos and sound on your own computer: every format, a smaller file, and a target size in MB when it has to fit. Nothing is uploaded.

  • A size, not a guess. Type 25 MB and it works out the bitrate from the length, for an email, a festival form or Discord.
  • Every format, one window. HEIC, PNG, ProRes, WAV in; JPG, WebP, H.265, AV1, MP3, GIF and stills out.
  • Nothing leaves the computer. No upload, no account; the originals stay as they are.
