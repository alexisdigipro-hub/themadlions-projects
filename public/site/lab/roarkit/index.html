01 · A look

See it for yourself.

The real app, with the demo's sample drives. Choose a screen.

What the computer needs, first.

RoarKit opens on a score for the computer: the start-up disk, the drives, the caches, memory, heat and battery, in a few seconds. Each thing to sort out comes with the button to the tool that does it.

Where did the space go?

A drive, your home folder, a shoot: every folder by its size, the biggest files, the space each kind of file takes, and the big files nobody has touched for months. On a Mac, the local Time Machine snapshots too.

Find the twins.

The same file twice whatever its name, the same video in another size, codec or format, the same sound in a WAV and its MP3, a trim cut out of a longer video. Previews side by side, the one to keep suggested.

A report of the clips.

Every clip of a card or a folder with a frame, its length, codec and profile, picture, frame rate, timecode and sound. Save it as a PDF for the post house or as a list for Excel.

Two panes, every detail.

Copy and move between two folders without ever writing over, compare them, make a project's folders from a template. Choose a file to see everything inside it: every stream, its colour, bit depth, timecode and camera.

The computer, live.

Every core of the processor, the graphics, memory and its pressure, swap, the drives, the network, heat and battery, and the programs that work it hardest. One button measures the internet: how long a 10 GB delivery takes to go up and come down.

How fast is this drive?

A test file is written, read back and removed; then you see which ProRes, BRAW, H.264 and H.265 rates the drive can record and play. A long write shows where an SSD slows down once its cache is full, as on a long take, and each drive keeps its last results to compare.

Your passwords, locked.

One master password opens them; they are sealed with AES-256 on this computer and never leave it. 2FA codes, a generator, weak, reused and old passwords marked, and an import from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, 1Password or Bitwarden.

Every drive, checked.

What each drive reports of its own health, its free space and how it is connected, with Eject in the list. A drive that warns it may fail moves to the top of the Overview.

Dark, light, your colour.

Dark, Light or Automatic with the computer, six accent colours, the text size, less movement. On a Mac the side menu is the system's own frosted glass.

05 · Safe

Your files, your call.

RoarKit only reads until you choose. Then it moves, never deletes.

Privacy PolicyTerms
Only the Trash

RoarKit never deletes your files: what you choose goes to the computer's Trash and waits there until you empty it.

One always stays

Of every group of twins one file stays, checked twice, on the page and in the app. Nothing in a Masters folder is ever offered.

Nothing written over

Copies, moves, archives and renames never write over a file: a name already taken gets a number, and a rename can be undone.

On your computer

Everything runs on your computer. It goes online only to look for an update and, when you press it, for the internet speed test.

A vault only you open

The passwords are sealed with AES-256 under your master password and never leave the computer. We cannot open them, and no one can bring a forgotten master password back.

07 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarKit free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no account.

What happened to RoarTwin?

It grew. RoarTwin found duplicates; RoarKit still does, as Find twins, next to fifteen other tools. If RoarTwin is on your computer, it updates itself into RoarKit and keeps your settings.

Can it delete something by mistake?

It does not delete your files at all. What you choose goes to the Trash, one of each group of twins always stays, and Masters folders are never touched. Empty the Trash only when you are sure.

Is Clean up safe?

It looks only in the folders where programs keep their caches, the render cache of Resolve, the media cache of Premiere, the browsers' and the like, and moves only what is inside them. The programs make them again when they need them. Your projects, media and renders are never touched.

How sure is a card copy?

Every copy is read back from the drive after it is written and compared with the card, file by file, with SHA-256. Each copy gets a report and an ASC MHL with MD5, which DITs and post houses read. Months later, Check a backup reads the drive again against that report.

Where are my passwords kept?

In one file on your computer, sealed with AES-256 under a key made from your master password. They are not sent anywhere and there is no cloud. You can save an encrypted backup for another drive. If the master password is forgotten, no one can open the vault, we neither.

Why can't it read the temperatures?

Computers do not let an app read their heat sensors without special rights. RoarKit shows what the system itself says, the heat level and whether it is slowing down to cool off, which is what you need to act on.

Does it work on external drives and servers?

Yes: any folder your computer can open, a card, a USB or Thunderbolt drive, a network share. Drive health needs the drive to pass its SMART on, which many USB cases and card readers do not.
