01 · Only here

Three things only RoarWriter does.

Why a director writes the next script here, and not in a word processor.

01

The keys of Final Draft.

Enter goes on to what usually comes next: after a scene heading the action, after a name the dialogue, after the dialogue the next name. Tab turns a line into another kind, Cmd or Ctrl and a number picks one. The format takes care of itself while you write.

02

Greek, as well as English.

Type ΕΣΩΤ. or ΕΞΩΤ. and the line becomes a scene heading, as INT. and EXT. do. Names, places and the time of day are suggested as you type, ΜΕΡΑ and ΝΥΧΤΑ included, and the PDF prints Greek in the same Courier as the rest.

02 · The page

A script that reads like a script.

The page as producers, actors and the crew expect it, and the files the rest of the industry uses.

The PDF

Ready to send.

Courier 12 with the standard margins, a title page, page numbers from page two, A4 or US Letter. The pages are laid out for real, so a scene heading never ends a page and a name stays with its lines.

2.3.
The files

Final Draft and Fountain.

Open a script from Final Draft or Fountain, save it back as either. Paste a screenplay as plain text and it turns into elements.

.fdx in · out.fountain in · out.pdf out
At the side

Scenes and characters.

Every scene numbered, the one you are in lit; one click takes you there. Every character with how many times they speak and in how many scenes.

Updates itself 0.1

Writes offline, too.

The Mac and Windows apps carry the whole program inside them: on a plane or on set with no signal, you go on writing, and the script is sent to your account when the connection is back. New versions download by themselves.

03 · Everything

And all a draft needs.

The rest of RoarWriter, briefly.

01Seven kinds of line

Scene heading, action, character, parenthetical, dialogue, transition and shot, each where the format puts it.

02It knows what you mean

INT. or ΕΣΩΤ. at the start of a line makes a scene heading; CUT TO: makes a transition. A parenthetical opens its own brackets.

03Title page

Title, credit, author, draft and date, contact: on the first page of the PDF and in the files.

04Page count as you go

How many pages the script is, always in the bar at the top.

05Undo, and nothing to save

Every change keeps itself. ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z go back and forth through what you did.

06Your library

Every script as a page of its own, with its pages and scenes; search, duplicate, delete.

07Import

Bring in a .fdx from Final Draft or a .fountain, title page included, and go on writing.

08A real menu

On Mac and Windows: Export PDF, Save as Final Draft or Fountain, and the seven kinds with their shortcuts.

09Spelling in two languages

The apps check Greek and English as you type, with suggestions on a right click.

10The look of RoarMe

Dark glass around a white page, round glass buttons, and the orange of RoarWriter.

11In the browser too

The same app on the web, nothing to install, with your scripts in your account.

12AI only when you ask

Nothing is sent to the AI until you press one of its buttons, and only the scene you are on.

06 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarWriter free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a RoarMe account: the same one as RoarMe, Roardino and RoarList. It is what keeps your scripts safe and the same on every device.

Can I open my Final Draft scripts?

Yes: import the .fdx and go on writing; save it back as .fdx whenever you need it in Final Draft. Fountain works both ways too.

Does it write Greek scripts properly?

Yes. Scene headings with ΕΣΩΤ. and ΕΞΩΤ., names in capitals, suggestions in Greek, spelling in Greek in the apps, and a PDF in the same format as an English script.

What does the AI see?

Only the scene you are on and a little of what comes before it, and only when you press one of its buttons. Its answer appears beside the page; nothing changes until you press Insert or Replace.

Does it work without internet?

The Mac and Windows apps do: you write as usual and the script is sent to your account when the connection is back. The AI needs a connection.

A4 or US Letter?

Either, chosen per script on its title page. The margins and the font stay those of the standard screenplay format.
