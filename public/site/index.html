Questions

Good to know.

What does The Mad Lions do?

The Mad Lions is a film production house. We make commercials, music videos, feature and short films, documentaries, corporate films and stage visuals, and finish them in house at Aibsent Studios, our post-production and AI studio. One team takes a film from the first idea to the final master.

Who is behind The Mad Lions?

The studio is led by producer and film director Alex Konstantinidis, with a team of assistant directors, filmmakers, editors, set designers and production assistants, and trusted crews for every shoot.

Which brands and artists have you worked with?

Brands such as Visa, L’Oréal, UNESCO, Aperol, Campari, Nescafé, Tresemmé, Axe, Wind, Stoiximan and Gree, and artists such as Michalis Hatzigiannis, Helena Paparizou, Antonis Remos, Nikos Oikonomopoulos, Natasa Theodoridou and Melisses, with Panik Records, Heaven Music and Universal Music.

How much does a commercial or a music video cost?

It depends on the idea, the shoot days, the cast and the post-production. We make films on budgets from under €5k to over €50k. Tell us yours and we come back with a concept, a treatment and a clear budget.

Can you finish a film we shot ourselves?

Yes. Aibsent Studios, our post-production and AI studio, takes on editing, color grading, visual effects, motion graphics and compositing for films shot by others, and makes films with AI.

Where do you shoot?

Wherever the film needs to be: in a studio or on location, with a tight crew and the right gear. We deliver masters for TV, cinema and every social format.

How do we start a project with you?

Write a few lines through the form on this page or to info@themadlions.com: what you are making, when you need it and your budget. We answer within a working day.
