Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarMe free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Do I need a phone number?

No. An email and a username are enough. People find you by your @name, or by the link and QR code you share.

How big can a file be?

Up to 50 MB straight in the chat, kept as long as the chat. Up to 3 GB with RoarTransfer, kept 3 days, with a link anyone can download from.

Who sees my location?

Only the chat you send it to, and only when you press Send. RoarMe does not follow where you are.

Where are my messages kept?

On our provider's servers in London, always sent over an encrypted connection. You can delete your account, and with it your profile and one-to-one chats, at any time.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. Sign in on the Mac, the PC, the browser and the phone at the same time; everything stays in step.
