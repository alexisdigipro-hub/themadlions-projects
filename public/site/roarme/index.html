Turn on end-to-end encryption in any chat, for as long as you need. The keys stay on your devices: nobody else can read what you write, not even us.

Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarMe free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Do I need a phone number?

No. An email and a username are enough. People find you by your @name, or by the link and QR code you share.

How big can a file be?

Up to 50 MB straight in the chat, kept as long as the chat. Up to 3 GB with RoarTransfer, kept 3 days, with a link anyone can download from.

Who sees my location?

Only the chat you send it to, and only when you press Send. RoarMe does not follow where you are.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted?

In a secret chat, yes. Open any chat or group, turn on Encrypted Chat and every message from then on is locked on your device; only the devices of the people in that chat can open it, not RoarMe. Turn it off whenever you like. For now it covers text; photos, files and voice messages wait until it is off. A device you add later cannot read the messages sent before it.

Where are my messages kept?

On our provider's servers in London, always sent over an encrypted connection, and in a secret chat locked end to end. You can delete your account, and with it your profile and one-to-one chats, at any time.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. Sign in on the Mac, the PC, the browser and the phone at the same time; everything stays in step.

Is RoarMe finished?

Not yet: RoarMe is in beta. It works every day, new things arrive all the time, and the apps tell you when a newer version is out. Tell us what you miss at info@themadlions.com.
