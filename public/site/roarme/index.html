06 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarMe free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Do I need a phone number?

No. An email and a username are enough. People find you by your @name, or by the link and QR code you share.

How big can a file be?

Up to 50 MB straight in the chat, kept as long as the chat. Up to 5 GB with RoarTransfer (5 GB kept 1 day, 3 GB kept 3 days, 1 GB kept 7 days), with one link anyone can download from; each file can be downloaded up to 5 times.

How do Tasks work?

Tap Tasks in the bar at the foot. Give a task to yourself or to anyone you share a chat with; for someone else it arrives as a request they accept or decline, then you both see its progress. Reminders and a calendar of the month live there too. Tasks never appear inside a conversation.

What is an ordino, and Roardino?

An ordino is the day's call sheet: call time, location, programme. Roardino makes them, and the camera button beside the clip opens it inside RoarMe, signed in with your RoarMe account. Send it and the crew gets a card that opens on any phone.

How do I get new versions?

In the browser and on the phone you always have the newest. The Mac and Windows apps from 1.4.9 download new versions themselves and ask for a quick restart; older ones show a card with a download.

Are my messages end-to-end encrypted?

In a secret chat, yes. Turn on Encrypted Chat in any chat or group and every message from then on is locked on your device; only the devices in that chat can read it.

Can I use it on several devices?

Yes. Sign in on the Mac, the PC, the browser and the phone at the same time; everything stays in step.
