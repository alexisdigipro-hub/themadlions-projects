02 · Only here

Three things only RoarList does.

Why a director stops keeping the shot list in a spreadsheet.

01

A frame for every shot.

Drop a picture on a shot, paste it with ⌘V, or choose one. Switch to Storyboard and the list becomes a board of frames, each with its number, size, angle, movement, lens and length. Frames follow the format you shoot in.

One PDF for the crew.

Print the list, or the storyboard, in one go: landscape, A4 or Letter, with the title, client, director, DoP, shoot day and notes on top and the page number at the bottom. The crew reads it on set, nobody needs an app.

Numbers that read on set.

04 · Everything

And all a shoot needs.

The rest of RoarList, briefly.

01Scenes and shots

A scene number like 12A and a heading like INT. KITCHEN - NIGHT. For each shot: size, angle, movement, lens, what we see, cast, length and notes, with the usual values a tap away.

02The project on top

Title, client, director, DoP, shoot day, format and general notes for the shoot, on screen and on every PDF.

03Ticked off on the day

Tick a shot when it is in the can. Shots done out of total and the running time show on top and beside every list.

04Drag to reorder

Drag a shot by its handle, inside its scene or into another one. Scenes move up and down with a button.

05Undo, and nothing to save

Every change saves itself as you type. ⌘Z brings back deletes, moves and pictures, up to 50 steps.

06Only the columns you use

Hide frame, size, angle, movement, lens, cast, length or notes from the list and the PDF. The details stay, ready to come back.

07Hand it over

Send a .roarlist file with the frames inside; on a computer with RoarList it opens with a double-click. Or save one web page that opens in any browser.

08All your lists at hand

A sidebar with a cover for each list that you can make wider with a drag, search with ⌘K, and a right-click to rename, duplicate, export or delete. The last list you had open opens again.

09From the keyboard

New shot, new scene, search, PDFs, undo and settings all have a shortcut, with a page that lists them (⌘/).

10Greek and English

The whole app, and the PDF, in either language, or as your system. Your scenes and notes are never touched.

11On the phone too

The browser version fits a phone screen or a narrow window: the lists slide in from the side.

12Try it first

A sample shot list for a night shoot, with drawn frames, opens with no account. Change anything; it resets when you leave.

07 · Questions

Good to know.

Is RoarList free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads.

Do I need an account?

Not on Mac and Windows: everything stays on your computer. In the browser you sign in with your RoarMe account, the same one as RoarMe, Roardino and RoarMail. The example needs nothing.

Are my lists the same on the computer and in the browser?

Not yet. The apps keep lists on the computer and the browser keeps them in your account. To move one, save it as a .roarlist file and open it on the other side. The RoarMe account is coming to the desktop apps too.

How do I give it to the crew?

As a PDF, the list or the storyboard. As a single web page that opens in any browser. Or as a .roarlist file, for someone who has RoarList, with all the frames inside.

Where do the frames come from?

From you: a still, a reference, a photo from the recce, a sketch you scanned. Drop it on the shot, paste it with ⌘V or choose it. PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF; big photos are made lighter so the list stays fast.

How are the shots numbered?

By the scene number and a letter: 1A, 1B, 1C. The letters I and O are skipped, because on a slate they read as 1 and 0. Move a shot and the numbers follow.

Which formats can I shoot in?

16:9, 2.39:1, 1.85:1, 4:3, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16. You can set the one new lists start with, and the lens a new list starts on, in Settings.
