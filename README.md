# THEMADLIONS Projects

Web-based film production workspace for The Mad Lions. Dark mode, desktop first, works on phones and tablets.

**Phase 1 (this repo):** everything runs in the browser and is saved in that browser (localStorage). Works on GitHub Pages with zero backend.
**Phase 2:** Supabase (auth, Postgres, storage, edge function for the AI) so the whole team logs in from anywhere. Schema is already in `supabase/schema.sql`.

## What is inside

- Projects in six categories: Music Video, Event, Editing, Ad, Visuals, IV
- Script import: PDF, Word (.docx), Final Draft (.fdx), Fountain, plain text, or paste. Pages files: export to PDF first
- Event category: no script, breakdown or shot list; the Schedule tab becomes a Run of show (event days with timed blocks and owners, standard blocks from load-in to load-out), call sheets show the run of show and talent calls, and progress counts venue, crew, run of show, event days and recap deliverables
- Music tab (Music Video projects only): upload the song (MP3, M4A, WAV, private Supabase bucket; a WAV, AIFF or FLAC is converted to a 192 kbps MP3 on your own device before it goes up, so a 40 MB master becomes about 6 MB in storage and the master never leaves your disk; the encoder is lamejs, LGPL, kept as its own file under public/vendor/lamejs and loaded only when needed), waveform player drawn in the browser with click-to-seek and section loops, a Download button that saves the stored file to the device, several versions (master, playback, instrumental), and a song map: sections (intro, verses, choruses) with start and end times, lyrics, notes and the setups from the breakdown that cover each one; paste the whole lyric to create sections at once, mark times while the song plays, export the map for the editor. Lyrics from the audio with OpenAI Whisper (own key in Settings): either writes the lyrics and creates sections at the pauses, or only times the sections you already pasted by matching what it heard. The AI treatment breakdown reads the song map and links setups to sections
- Script revisions: every new upload or edited save keeps the previous version with its revision colour (White, Blue, Pink, Yellow, Green…), side-by-side compare, restore. Re-running scene detection matches scenes by heading so breakdown tags, shots and schedule survive, and changed scenes are flagged on the strips
- Scene detection in English and Greek (INT./EXT., ΕΣΩΤ./ΕΞΩΤ., DAY/NIGHT, ΜΕΡΑ/ΝΥΧΤΑ), characters, page eighths
- Treatment, concept and moodboard breakdown: upload a director's treatment, a concept in plain words, a PDF moodboard with images or reference photos; Claude groups the material into shootable setups (location, time of day, talent, wardrobe, props, art, effects, equipment, look, time estimate), lists locations, talent and producer notes, and can draft a first shot list. Built for music videos and commercials that never had a screenplay
- AI breakdown with Claude: props, wardrobe, vehicles, SFX, extras, flags per scene, plus reports by character, location and element. CSV export
- Production sheet (Breakdown > Sheet): the grid you used to rebuild by hand in a spreadsheet. Scenes down the page, characters across it, a dot where someone plays. Group the scenes by **set**, by **shooting day**, by **day or night**, or leave them in script order; each group shows its scene count and page total, and the bottom row says how many scenes each person is in. The scene and set columns stay pinned while you scroll sideways. Download .csv to open it in Google Sheets or print it
- Shot list per scene: size, angle, movement, gear, lens, camera, storyboard frames, list and board views, CSV and print
- Shot list **Customise** (ideas taken from Shot Lister): **Columns** in any order, renamed, each on or off separately for the screen, the printout and the share link, plus **your own columns** (props, wardrobe, VFX, anything; each gets a field in the shot form); **Pick lists** for sizes, angles, movements, gear and your own lens kit, one per line; default **setup and shoot minutes**; **Look**: colour of headings (black, project colour or any), text size on paper and on the link, link light or dark. Saved per project; an administrator can make it the company layout. Every shot has **Setup** and **Shoot** minutes.
- **Shoot day** view of the shot list: pick a shoot day and its scenes' shots line up in shooting order, reorder them with ↑ ↓, add Lunch, Company move or a Break, and every shot gets a planned start and end from the shooting call on the call sheet. The head shows the planned last shot, how it sits against the day's wrap, shots done, and, while shooting, **Live**: press **Done** as each shot is in the can, the time is stamped, and the head says how many minutes ahead or behind you are; on today's date the shot you should be on now is highlighted. On a phone each shot is a card with a big Done button.
- **Share link** on the shot list (needs the Share permission to edit, like other links): from List or Storyboard it shares every scene with its shots, from Shoot day it shares that day in shooting order with times; only the columns switched on for the link go into it. Opens on a phone without login, each shot a card; the links show up in Share under Everything.
- Stripboard schedule: shoot days, scene assignment, industry strip colours, unscheduled pool
- Day Out of Days for cast (SW / W / WF / SWF / H) computed from the stripboard
- Share link: one tap turns the day's call sheet into a public link that opens on any phone without login. Because the link carries everyone's phone number and links get forwarded, Settings > Call sheets can put a **six digit access code** on every call sheet link: the code goes out with the link, the page shows nothing without it, and crew type it once per phone. Delivery and estimate pages take an optional code of their own from their form, laid out for mobile (big call time, location with directions, pinned note, everyone's call, scenes). Department requirements stay private. Sharing again after edits refreshes the same link
- Send message: the call sheet as a WhatsApp-ready message (whole sheet for the group, or a personal message per cast and crew member with only their call time and location), mail with everyone in Bcc, or copy. WhatsApp links on cast and crew cards
- Weekly backups (administrators, Settings > Data): once `supabase/backups.sql` is run, the database takes a snapshot of the whole workspace every Monday at 06:00 Athens time and keeps the last twelve, inside Supabase, no credentials anywhere. Back up now takes one by hand. Download turns a snapshot into the same file the Restore button reads, so a copy can live on your own disk; Restore puts projects, calendar items and the library back
- Finance overview statistics: a **Sort by** bar above the By project, By client, By category and By project type tables (Income, Expense, Profit, Margin, Share, Outstanding, Owed, Name, plus High to low / Low to high) sorts all four at once; a click on any column heading does the same. The tables show each row's share of the year's income or expenses and a totals row; the client table adds Outstanding (invoiced, not yet paid, gross). **By project | By month** are two tabs of the same box. By month shows one line per month with its income, expenses, owed, profit and margin, each amount in the month it is dated (so the months match the bars above; a client budget still to invoice sits on the project's shoot day); click a month to see the projects behind it. Owed amounts follow the year picked at the top, so a project from another year no longer shows up with only its owed fees. A **By quarter** table gives income, expense, profit and the VAT charged, paid and balance per calendar quarter, what the quarterly VAT return settles. The Profit tile compares the booked profit with last year's.
- Finance categories are yours to shape too: **Finance → Settings → Income & expense categories** (administrators). Two lists; click a name to rename it and every transaction and recurring item filed under it follows, and for an expense column the budget categories that land in it follow as well. Reorder, add, remove (a category with items on it asks where they go first), Reset to standard. Saves at once.
- Budget categories are yours to shape: **Settings > Budget** (administrators) lists the groups (Above the line, Production, Post-production, Other) and the categories in each. Click a name to rename it and every budget line already filed under it, in every project, follows. Add, reorder, remove; a category with lines on it asks where those lines go before it disappears. Each category names the Finance column a payment on it lands in. Reset to standard brings the built-in list back; lines under a category that no longer exists show as "Unlisted" in the budget rather than vanishing
- My work, Add job: **Project** comes first. Pick one and the client (or the project's title when it has no client), the description (the title) and the shooting date fill in from it: the latest day already shot, else the first one coming, else the project's start date. Anything you typed by hand stays; switching projects replaces only what the previous one filled
- My work to the budget: a job someone enters in their own My work **with a project** puts their fee into that project's budget as a line paid to them (category from their department when it matches, else Production staff). If the production already entered a line paying them, the new job is tied to that line instead (amount and paid state follow the line), and a second job on the same project is refused, so nothing is entered twice. A job that comes from a budget is never marked paid by hand: it turns to Paid when the payment is recorded (Finance, the budget's Pay, or Record payment in Team work), which writes Finance, the budget and the job at once. The job is linked to the line, so a payment turns it to paid. Members without Budget = edit go through `supabase/worklog_budget.sql`, a function that can add one line for themselves and nothing else
- Budget line paid to a team member: **Paid to** on a budget line lists the team (name and position from their profile) or "someone outside the team" with a free vendor name. Pick a member and the line goes straight into **their My work** as a pending job with the line's amount, the project, the client and a **work date** you set on the line. Change the amount and it follows; delete the line and the job goes. Record a payment from Finance or Pay and the job turns to paid on the day it was settled, with the method. The member sees only their own line, never the rest of the budget, and the job says "from the project budget". Administrators write into anyone's My work directly; a producer with Budget = edit needs `supabase/worklog_assign.sql` run once
- Tabs per project: a **new project opens with Overview alone**. Overview > Edit details > **Tabs** lists every tab the project's category offers; tap one to switch it on, tap again to hide it. Overview always stays. Changing the category brings its own tabs in switched off. Nothing is deleted, a hidden tab keeps its data and its page still opens from an old link. Projects made before this keep every tab
- A project's tabs today: **Overview** (progress, Tasks, the Song player for a Music Video, and Files/Links/Production notes, all framed cards, no separate Files & notes tab any more), **Script & Breakdown** (the breakdown lives framed inside Script now), **Shot list**, **Schedule & Sheets** (call sheets are framed inside Schedule now), **Reports** (opt-in, off by default), **Budget**, **Post** (opt-in, off by default), **Project Database** (Cast, Crew, Locations and Equipment, framed cards in columns where they fit). The project's own Calendar and Chat tabs are gone: the sidebar Calendar covers every project already, and a project's conversation is the same room as the sidebar Chat
- Projects page: a project's name is two fields in New / Edit project, **Artist / Client** and **Title**, saved together as "Artist - Title" (what the header, chat, finance and call sheets show; projects named before the split are read by cutting at the first " - "). On a computer each card is a square cover (category and progress on it) with only the name under it, centred: the artist on one line, the title under it (a very long one ends in … and shows in full on hover); on a phone the compact 3-per-row cards keep the status too. **Duplicate** (on a card's Edit / Duplicate / × on hover, or next to Edit details on a project's Overview, which then opens the copy) makes a full copy named "… (copy)" with the next project code: script, breakdown, shots, schedule, call sheets, budget, cast, crew, locations, gear, tasks, notes, photos and songs, each file copied into the new project so deleting from one never touches the other. Budget payments stay with the original (the copy's lines show the same amounts, unpaid); the copy starts unlocked. Calendar events and chat messages are not copied. Delivered projects leave the main grid. A **Delivered · N** chip at the end of the category row, in the inverse colour of the others, shows them alone, grey until hovered; the category chips count only what is shown. The heading counts active and delivered separately.
- Project cover image (compressed, stored in the project) shown on the dashboard card, the project header, the Overview and the call sheet; Overview progress bar with weighted stages per category (script, breakdown, budget, cast, locations, shot list, schedule, shoot days reported, post and delivery; Editing projects use cut, approval and deliverables), each stage linking to its tab; dashboard cards carry a thin progress bar
- Transcript from the audio shown in the Script tab of music video projects, with copy and "use as script text"
- Home: the team strip (who is in, who is off today) above the Projects grid, see further down
- Project files: uploads to a private Supabase bucket (50 MB per file on the free plan) with open and download, plus a cloud folder link per project (pCloud, Drive, Dropbox) for footage and masters

- **Customise** (button on a call sheet, for whoever can edit call sheets): set the call sheet up your way. **Sections**: put them in any order with ↑ ↓, rename any of them (Cast can be "Artists", Scenes can be "Sets"), and tick separately whether each shows on the printed sheet and on the share link; **Add your own section** for anything else (catering, dress code, safety rules), with a text for every day that you can type over on the sheet for one day only. **Details**: cover picture, key crew, the line under the call, weather, sunrise and sunset, phone numbers, Parking, Nearest hospital, the Lunch / break time, the Shooting call time and the Est. wrap time, each on or off for the sheet and for the link (lunch and wrap start off on the link, the shooting call starts off on both). **Words**: rename General crew call, Shooting call, Lunch and Est. wrap. **Look**: header in three columns or with the title and the call on top, headings and call time in black, the project colour or any colour you pick, text size for the sheet and for the link, link light or dark. This layout is saved for the project; an administrator can make it the layout of every project that has none of its own, and a project can go back to the company layout. **This day only**: a different title, general call and wrap, location name, address, who to ask and their phone, extra people who are not in the project (cast or crew, with their call), and anyone left out of the day (× next to them on the sheet; Show again in Customise). Call times of each person can be typed straight on the sheet. Anything switched off for the link is left out of the link's data, not just hidden. Press Share link again after changes so the link shows them; links made before this keep their old look.
- **Calls by group** section on the call sheet: one row per group with its own call (Production crew 10:00, Beauty crew 09:00, Artist, Dancers, Cast, Model…), typed by hand per shoot day; Add a group, or Add the usual groups to start from those six; ↑ ↓ and ×. On the link it is its own card, group on the left and the time big on the right; in the WhatsApp message each line reads "⏰ GROUP: time". Rename it (e.g. ORDINO), move it or switch it off in Customise. Layouts saved before it existed get it right under the Note.

- **Link name** (call sheet and shot list links): after Share link, type a name under the link and press Use this name; the address ends in it instead of random letters, e.g. `…/#/s/xartines-agapes-day-1`. - Budget top sheet: lines grouped by category, each with a Description (optional: left empty, the line takes its category's name, so a crew line needs only category, person and amount) and a Name (who is paid), one amount per line, a VAT column that says "VAT included" when the line has one, paid and balance from the payments recorded in Finance. Euros only, no contingency. - Finance → **Invoices** (administrators): build and keep real invoices. Your own company side (name, address, VAT number, bank, website, the "reason for not charging VAT" note, the BGN/EUR exchange rate, the running number and prefix) is filled in once under Finance → Settings → **Invoice profile**, where you also upload a **stamp & signature PNG**. Each new invoice starts from that profile, so you only type the client (name, address, VAT number) and the service lines (description, project, date, amount); the net, VAT, total, the amount **in words** and the BGN column are worked out for you. Pick VAT 0 and the reason note prints; set a percentage and it is added. Status is Draft / Sent / Paid, each invoice can link to a project, and **Download PDF** makes a clean A4 with the line table, totals, bank details, Provider/Recipient blocks and your stamp in the signature box. **Preview** opens that same PDF in a new tab first. **Share link** publishes a public page (no login) at a link you send the client yourself — it shows a preview of the invoice and its own **Download PDF** button; the link is copied to your clipboard automatically, and sharing the same invoice again reuses the same link with the refreshed details. The running number advances on its own; Duplicate copies an old invoice as a fresh draft. A copy of your company details is frozen onto each invoice when you save it, so changing the profile later never rewrites an invoice already sent. The toolbar has **Company & stamp** (upload once: a **letterhead PNG** — your logo with the details, printed centred at the top of every invoice — and the **stamp/signature PNG** for the Provider box; both apply to every invoice, including ones made earlier), **Clients** (save a client's name, address, VAT, email, phone; pick it in the editor to fill the recipient) and **Services** (save a name, description and default price; pick it to drop a ready line in). Each line has a Description, a **Project** and an optional **Date** (the shoot day), and one amount — no quantity. The PDF is drawn in the site's font in a modern layout: your letterhead on top, a large "Invoice" title with the number and date, Billed by / Billed to, a clean Amount (EUR) / Amount (BGN) table with project and date under each line, a light grey **Total due** card, the bank details in a light box, and your stamp above the Provider signature line. Administrators only, stored in the finance table; **re-run `supabase/finance.sql`** once so the database accepts the new invoice rows.

Setup is on the settings panel itself: a Google Cloud OAuth Client ID (Web application type, the redirect URI shown there), the Client ID and Secret as the function's secrets, the function deployed in Supabase (name `google-calendar`), Connect (a refresh token comes back once, copied into the function's secrets too), then pick the calendar. ## Deploy on GitHub Pages

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (for example `themadlions-projects`).
2. Push this folder to the `main` branch.
3. In the repository: **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: GitHub Actions**.
4. The workflow in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` builds and publishes on every push. The site appears at `https://<your-user>.github.io/themadlions-projects/`.

Routing uses hash URLs (`/#/p/...`) so it works on Pages without server rewrites.

## AI breakdown key (Phase 1)

Settings → AI breakdown → paste an Anthropic API key. It is stored only in that browser and calls the API directly. In Phase 2 the key moves to a Supabase Edge Function.

## Phase 2: Supabase (team logins, one shared database)

1. Create a project at supabase.com (region Frankfurt).
2. SQL Editor → New query → paste all of `supabase/schema.sql` → Run. Then the same with `supabase/storage.sql` (photo bucket) `supabase/library.sql` (company library), `supabase/todos.sql` (general tasks), `supabase/audio.sql` (song files), `supabase/files.sql` (project files), `supabase/finance.sql` (administrators-only finance) `supabase/chat.sql` (team chat) `supabase/notices.sql` (notices) `supabase/worklog.sql` (personal work logs), `supabase/drives.sql` (drives archive) `supabase/shares.sql` (public call sheet links), `supabase/activity.sql` (activity log), `supabase/deliveries.sql` (client replies on a delivery page) `supabase/share_access.sql` (only members with the Share permission may send delivery links) `supabase/share_track.sql` (counts openings, closes a link by hand or on a date), `supabase/estimates.sql` (lets a client accept a cost estimation), `supabase/share_pin.sql` (an access code on a public link) `supabase/notices_ack.sql` (a notice can only be edited by whoever sent it; Got it goes through a narrow function) `supabase/worklog_assign.sql` (a budget line paid to a colleague reaches their My work even when the person editing the budget is not an administrator) `supabase/worklog_budget.sql` (a job in My work adds the person's own fee to the project budget), `supabase/chat_rooms.sql` (chat rooms: project rooms, groups, direct messages, replies, attachments), `supabase/chat_project_members.sql` (an administrator can take people out of a project's conversation), `supabase/usage.sql` (database and storage sizes for Settings > Usage) and `supabase/backups.sql` (weekly snapshots of the workspace with pg_cron, kept in a backups table; if it says pg_cron is not available, enable the extension from Database > Extensions first).
3. Authentication → Providers → Email: keep it enabled; turn **Confirm email** off if you want teammates to sign in immediately.
4. Project Settings → API: copy the Project URL and the publishable (anon) key into `src/lib/supabaseConfig.js`, commit, push. The site rebuilds in remote mode.
5. Open the site, create your account. The first account becomes the administrator and the workspace is created automatically.
6. Settings → Storage → "Import them into the team workspace" moves the projects from this browser's local mode into the database.
7. Team → Add teammate: enter their email and permissions. They create an account with that email and land straight in the workspace.

Data model: each project is one JSON document (`projects.data`), events one row each, members and invites per workspace. Row Level Security enforces workspace membership, per-project access and edit rights; per-module view/edit levels are applied by the app. Live updates come through Supabase Realtime.

The AI key stays in each browser (Settings) for now; moving it into a Supabase Edge Function is the next step.
